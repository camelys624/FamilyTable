```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: One finished square WeChat mini program app icon for a warm Chinese family meal planning app, "家常 / Family Table". This is the actual icon artwork, not a presentation board or a device mockup.
Primary request: Redesign the brand icon to be extremely simple, quiet, warm and legible at 40 pixels, matching the app's creamy off-white and pale sage-green backgrounds, deep forest-green outlines, and restrained flat food-and-family illustrations.
Background: A perfectly solid pale sage green #EAF2EA filling the entire square image from edge to edge. No transparent background, no corner rounding baked into the file.
Subject: A SINGLE compact abstract family dining-table emblem. A simple round tabletop seen from slightly above with just TWO small rounded chair backs opposite one another; one small ivory plate with a warm butter-yellow center on the table. Distill the table into a few broad rounded silhouettes, not a furniture drawing. The tabletop, chair backs and plate must form one cohesive, balanced mark. Minimal structure, no separate decorative motifs.
Style: Refined flat illustration with clean gently organic rounded shapes and bold consistent deep forest-green #18332F outlines. Calm and friendly, like a minimal printed illustration in a modern family recipe notebook. Smooth flat opaque color, no sketch scribbles, no texture.
Palette: Only pale sage #EAF2EA for background, muted sage #A8BD9F for the main table shape, ivory #FFFDF7 for the plate, a small butter-yellow #F4C95D accent, and dark forest #18332F for outlines. Green and ivory dominate; yellow is only a small accent.
Composition: A square 1:1 full-bleed icon. One centered mark occupying about 62% of the image width, generous balanced breathing room all around. Every part stays inside a central circular safe area. Use thick enough outlines and large enough simplified forms to remain clear at tiny app icon sizes. Favor immediate recognition and a distinctive silhouette over detail.
Text: Absolutely no text, letters, Chinese characters, slogans, numbers or watermark.
Avoid: multiple plates, food illustrations, vegetables, utensils, extra chairs, people, smiling faces, house roofs, leaves, hearts, stars, steam, border frames, badge circles, busy patterns, gradients, shadows, gloss, 3D, photorealism, intricate legs or wood grain, multiple alternatives in the image, captions, phone screens. Do not copy the app page screenshot; design only the standalone icon.
```
